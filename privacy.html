BoxUp Labels: privacy

BoxUp Labels is the internal shipping label tool of BoxUp Luxury Gifting. It is used only by BoxUp staff and partners that BoxUp logistics approves.

What we get when you sign in with Google: your name, email address and Google profile photo. Nothing else from your Google account is read.

What we use it for: to know who is using the tool, to let BoxUp logistics give you access and Shree Anjani AWB numbers, and to record who printed which label.

Where it is kept: in BoxUp's own Google Sheet, which only BoxUp logistics can open. It is not sold or shared with anyone else.

Removing it: ask BoxUp logistics (logistics@boxupgifting.com) and your entry is removed.
